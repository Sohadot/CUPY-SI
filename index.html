01 / The personal thesis

From powerful systems to meaningful personal use.

A useful personal intelligence system would need to understand context, respect agency, adapt to diverse needs, and remain intelligible to the people using it. Superintelligence raises the ambition — and the need for responsible design.

Cupy is a short, memorable, consumer-facing name. Its identity is intentionally evocative rather than an invented technical acronym.
